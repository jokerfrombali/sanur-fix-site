How to Describe a Plumbing Issue When Booking a Sanur Plumber

When something leaks, clogs, or stops working at your Sanur villa, the fastest way to get help is to send clear photos and a short text on WhatsApp. You do not need to diagnose the fault yourself – but a few good shots and two honest sentences save everyone a wasted trip around the compound. Here is exactly what to capture and how to structure your message before you hit send.

What to Shoot in Photos and Video

Start with one wide shot of the whole area so the technician can see where the problem sits relative to walls, tanks, or the pool edge. Then move closer for a tight shot of the actual leak, corrosion, or broken fitting. If water is running, hold the phone steady for ten seconds of video; sound helps distinguish a drip from a pressure burst under PDAM supply lines.

In older Sanur villas you will often find galvanized pipes that have rusted through near the rooftop tank, or sea-air salt eating away at brass fittings by the pool pump. Photograph those joints from two angles if they look pitted. For septic-tank smells coming up through floor drains, film the drain itself plus any nearby vent pipe on the roof.

  • Wide context shot (whole bathroom, utility room, or pool deck)
  • Close-up of the leaking joint, cracked tile, or corroded fitting
  • 10-second video while water is running – include the sound
  • Photo of the meter or pressure gauge if it is visible
  • For pool issues: the water line, skimmer basket, and pump housing

Writing a Short Description

Two to three sentences are enough. State what happened, when you first noticed it, and anything you already tried. For example: 'Kitchen sink has been dripping slowly since Tuesday morning. I tightened the P-trap nut but it keeps weeping. No flooding yet.' That gives the technician a clear picture before he even picks up his toolbox.

If the problem appeared during the rainy season, mention that. Heavy downpours in Sanur can shift soil around external pipes, overload a small septic system, or push debris into an open pool return. A single line like 'started after last night's storm' saves the tech from guessing.

Villa Access Details

Most Sanur villas sit behind a compound wall with a gate, sometimes two gates (car plus pedestrian). Tell the technician whether the gate is locked, who holds the key, and whether your staff member will be home to let him in. If there is a separate pool gate or a utility room on the back of the property, note that too so he does not wander looking for the pump.

If no one is present, say so plainly and offer the gate code or arrange for a neighbour to unlock. The technician cannot quote accurately if he has to wait twenty minutes outside while you are at the beach.

Language Does Not Matter Much

Write in English, Bahasa Indonesia, or a mix of both – whichever feels natural. Technicians in Sanur deal with expat villa owners every day and understand common plumbing vocabulary: 'leak,' 'clog,' 'pressure drop,' 'chlorine smell.' You do not need perfect grammar. A photo labelled 'this pipe, dripping' is perfectly clear.

If you are more comfortable typing in Russian or another language, send it alongside an English keyword or a short translation from your phone. The goal is mutual understanding, not a polished report.

A Simple Message Template

Copy this structure into WhatsApp and fill in the blanks. It takes about two minutes and covers everything the technician needs before arriving:

  • Hi – I have a problem at my villa in [area/street], Sanur.
  • What's happening: [one sentence].
  • When it started: [today / 3 days ago / after the storm].
  • Photos/video attached below.
  • Access: [gate code / staff name / no one home, call me on arrival].
  • Best time to come: [morning / afternoon / flexible].
  • Please let me know if you can handle it and what the quote looks like.

Questions

Do I need to shut off the water before sending photos?

Only if water is actively flooding the floor or pooling near electrical outlets. In that case, close the main PDAM valve (usually under the kitchen sink or by the rooftop tank) and unplug any nearby appliances. Then send your photos. If you smell gas or see sparks near water, stop touching anything and call a professional immediately.

Can I get a price just from the photos I send?

Often yes for straightforward jobs like a leaking tap washer or a clogged drain. For anything involving hidden pipes, septic lines, or pool plumbing behind walls, the technician may need to inspect in person first. The final quote always depends on the specific job; he will confirm after seeing your photos or arriving at the villa.

What should I do if my pool water turns green overnight?

Take a photo of the water surface and the pump/filter area, note when it last looked clear, and check whether the chlorine level was tested recently. Do not mix different chemical products to 'fix' it quickly – combining chlorine with acids or other treatments can release harmful gas. Send the photos and describe what you added, then wait for the technician's guidance before dosing again.
