Humidity in Your Sanur Villa: Dehumidifiers & Ventilation

Bali's air is thick with moisture, and in Sanur the sea breeze makes it worse. If you notice condensation on windows, a musty smell in the bedroom, or clothes that never fully dry, your indoor humidity has crept past comfortable levels. The good news: a few practical steps — the right dehumidifier, smart ventilation habits, and proper AC use — can bring things back under control without a full renovation.

What Is 'Normal' Humidity?

Most people feel most comfortable between 40 % and 60 % relative humidity. Below 40 %, skin and eyes start to dry out; above 60 %, mould spores multiply quickly and fabrics stay damp for hours. In a typical Sanur villa the problem is rarely a leak — it is simply the tropical climate pushing warm ocean air through every open window. During the rainy months (roughly November to March) outdoor humidity regularly sits at 85–95 %. Old villas built before modern sealing are especially vulnerable because plaster walls and concrete floors wick moisture inward all day long.

A simple digital hygrometer from any hardware shop will tell you where you stand. Check it in the worst room first, usually the bathroom or a ground-floor bedroom facing east.

Choosing and Using a Dehumidifier

A compressor-type dehumidifier works best in Bali's hot, humid conditions. Look for a unit rated around 20–30 litres per day for a standard living area of 25–40 m²; larger open-plan villas may need two units or a higher-capacity model. Place it on a hard floor near the centre of the room, away from direct sunlight, with at least 15 cm clearance on each side so the intake grille can breathe.

Run it continuously during the wet months rather than switching it off overnight — that is when condensation forms fastest. Empty the tank daily or connect a drain hose into a nearby floor waste. Because Sanur air carries salt particles, wipe the exterior and inspect the filter mesh every two weeks to prevent corrosion. If you hear rattling or smell burnt plastic, switch the unit off immediately and have a technician check the compressor before using it again.

  • Set the target to 50–55 % RH and let the auto-shutoff do its job.
  • In very damp rooms (bathroom, laundry), run the unit with the door closed for an hour before opening it back up.
  • Never place a dehumidifier near a gas stove or any open flame.

Ventilation That Actually Helps

The instinct to throw every window wide open feels right, but in Sanur's sea breeze that simply pumps humid air through your villa all day. The trick is timing. Open windows and interior doors for 20–30 minutes early in the morning, before 8 am, when the air has cooled slightly and wind direction shifts away from the ocean. Close them once the afternoon heat builds.

During the rainy season keep ground-floor windows shut and rely on extraction fans instead. Make sure the bathroom fan runs at least 15 minutes after a shower so moisture is pulled out rather than pushed into the hallway. If your kitchen lacks a range hood, a small inline ducted fan venting straight outside will cut cooking-humidity build-up dramatically.

  • Check that bathroom and kitchen extraction fans still spin freely; a seized bearing is a common fault in older villas.
  • If you have a rooftop water tank, make sure its lid seals properly so rain-driven humidity does not seep down through the ceiling void.

Using Your Air Conditioner Wisely

A split-unit AC in 'dry' mode removes moisture without dropping the temperature as much as cool mode, which makes it useful on muggy evenings when you do not want the room too cold. Set the thermostat around 26 °C; going lower increases energy use without meaningfully improving comfort in this climate.

Clean or replace the filter every two weeks during the wet season — a clogged filter reduces airflow and can cause water to drip from the indoor unit onto your floor. If standing water pools under the outdoor condenser, check the drain line for a blockage before the next storm. And a safety note: if you smell gas near any appliance while working with electrics and water, stop what you are doing, open windows, and call a licensed technician. Do not attempt to repair wiring yourself.

Protecting Clothes and Belongings

Mould loves warm, damp fabric. In Sanur's climate, cotton shirts left in an unventilated wardrobe for a weekend can develop dark spots. Hang freshly washed clothes outside in direct sun rather than drying them indoors, and store seasonal clothing in breathable cotton bags instead of sealed plastic bins. Tuck a small silica-gel packet (refillable ones work well) into drawers and shoe boxes.

Cedar blocks or sachets absorb some moisture and deter moths, which thrive in humid conditions. For leather items, keep them away from walls that face the sea breeze and dust them monthly so salt residue does not dry on the surface. If you already see black speckles on a jacket or book, isolate it in a sealed bag outdoors and do not bring it back inside until it is fully dry.

Questions

How many litres per day dehumidifier do I need for my Sanur villa?

As a rough guide, 20–30 L/day covers a single room of about 30 m². Open-plan living areas or ground-floor bedrooms facing the ocean may need more. The exact figure depends on your layout and how much old plaster wicks moisture inward — send photos of the space on WhatsApp and we can suggest a suitable capacity.

Is it better to run the AC all night or use a dehumidifier?

For pure comfort at night, a dehumidifier set to 50 % RH plus a ceiling fan uses less energy than an AC running at 22 °C all night. In the wettest months, combining both works well: let the dehumidifier handle moisture while the AC runs on a timer for two hours before bed.

My bathroom has mould behind the mirror even though I clean it weekly. What else should I check?

The extraction fan may be recirculating air instead of venting outside, or the duct could be blocked with dust. Also check that the silicone seal around the shower tray is intact; hairline cracks let steam migrate into the wall cavity. If you are not comfortable opening the fan housing yourself, send a photo and we will point you in the right direction.
