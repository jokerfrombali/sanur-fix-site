What's different in Seminyak

Seminyak is dense: villas close together, older infrastructure and busy rentals. Quick fixes between guests are the norm.

Older villas

Many villas are 15–25 years old: tired pipework, old heaters and worn pool equipment.

Busy rentals

High occupancy means blocked toilets, broken mixers and hot-water complaints need fixing between check-ins.

Street flooding

Heavy rain floods lanes and overloads drains and septic systems.

Tight spaces

Small plots and shared walls make leak detection and pump rooms harder to reach.
