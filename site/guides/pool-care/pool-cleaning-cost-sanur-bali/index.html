There is no single fixed rate for pool cleaning in Sanur because every villa is different – size, water condition, filter type, and access all shift the final number. In practice, you pay for labour plus any chemicals or parts needed that day. The best way to get an accurate figure is to send a couple of photos on WhatsApp and let the technician quote from what they see.

What Is Actually Included in a Pool Clean

A standard visit covers the basics: skimming the surface, brushing walls and steps, vacuuming the bottom, emptying and rinsing the skimmer baskets, back-flushing or cleaning the filter, and testing the water (pH, chlorine, alkalinity). If the level is low, topping up with PDAM or well water is part of the job.

Sanur sits right on the coast, so salt spray and humidity push algae growth faster than inland areas. You will notice green film on the tiles within a few days if nothing is done, especially during the rainy season when wind-driven rain stirs up settled debris.

  • Skim, brush, and vacuum the full basin
  • Clean or back-flush the sand/DE cartridge filter
  • Test pH, free chlorine, and total alkalinity; adjust as needed
  • Empty skimmer baskets and clean the return jets
  • Check pump seal and pressure gauge for obvious leaks

One-Off Deep Clean vs Regular Maintenance

If you just moved into a villa or the pool has been sitting through the wet months (roughly November to March), you probably need a one-off deep clean first. That means draining a portion of the water, scrubbing heavy biofilm off the tiles, flushing the filter media, and rebalancing chemistry from scratch. It takes longer and costs more than a routine visit.

After that, most Sanur owners go to a weekly or fortnightly schedule. The technician arrives, does the standard checklist above, tops up chemicals, and leaves a short note on what they adjusted. Sticking to a regular rhythm keeps the water clear with far less effort than waiting until it turns cloudy.

What Drives the Price Up or Down

Pool volume is the biggest factor – a 4 × 8 m lap pool needs roughly twice the time and product of a small plunge pool. If your villa also has an attached spa or jacuzzi, that is usually quoted separately.

Other variables: how badly the water is out of balance when the tech arrives, whether the filter cartridge needs replacing, if the pump housing is corroded (common in older Sanur villas near the sea), and physical access. A pool tucked behind a garden wall with a narrow equipment shed will take longer to service than one open to the terrace.

Chemicals: Included or Extra?

Some operators bundle a basic set (chlorine tablets, pH down/up) into every visit; others charge per litre used. Ask before you book so there are no surprises at the end.

Bali tap water from PDAM can be soft in some parts of Sanur and harder where the supply mixes with well water. That changes how much chlorine the water actually holds. A technician who tests on-site will adjust dosing accordingly rather than guessing from a label.

One safety note: never mix different chlorine products or add acid while chlorine is still active. If you smell strong fumes or feel your eyes sting, step back, ventilate the area, and call the technician before adding anything else.

How to Choose a Pool Service You Can Trust

Start simple. Send two or three photos – the whole basin, the equipment area, and any visible problem (green water, cracked tile, leaking fitting). A reliable local tech will reply with what they see, what they would do, and a clear quote. No obligation until you say yes.

Ask whether they bring their own vacuum head, test kit, and chemicals, or if you need to stock them. Confirm they cover your specific stretch of Sanur (the eastern tip near Batu Bolong has different access than the western end near Sanur Beach). And check that they handle small repairs – a stuck skimmer door or a weeping pump seal – so you are not juggling two contractors for one pool.

  • Request a written list of what each visit includes
  • Confirm response window for urgent issues (leak, pump failure)
  • Make sure they carry spare O-rings, gaskets, and filter cartridges on site

Questions

How often should I get my pool cleaned in Sanur?

During the dry season (April–October) fortnightly is usually enough for a well-balanced pool. In the rainy months, weekly visits keep up with leaves, rainwater dilution, and faster algae growth. If you travel for more than ten days, book a clean before you leave and one when you return.

Do I need to be home while the technician works?

No. Most villa owners give a gate code or ask the staff to let the tech in. You will receive a short message after the visit summarising what was done and any chemical adjustments made. Just make sure the equipment area is unlocked and accessible.

Can the same person fix a leaking pump or replace a cracked tile?

Minor mechanical work – replacing an O-ring, swapping a cartridge, tightening a union – is typically handled during a service call. Larger jobs like re-plastering a section or replacing the pump motor are quoted separately from photos.
