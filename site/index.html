Local knowledge

Plumbing in Sanur is different

Most call-outs in Sanur come down to the same local conditions. Knowing them is half the diagnosis — that's why I work in one area only.

Villas built 15–20 years ago

A lot of Sanur villas still run on their original PVC pipework and fittings. Pipes that sit in the sun turn brittle, and joints start to weep. Often it's smarter to replace a section than to patch it again.

Salt air near the beach

In Sindhu, Semawang and Mertasari the sea air eats into taps, shower heads, hinges and pump motors much faster than inland. Choosing the right materials saves repeat repairs.

Well water and PDAM

Many villas combine PDAM mains with a well. Close to the sea, well water can be brackish — filters and cartridges clog faster and heaters build scale.
