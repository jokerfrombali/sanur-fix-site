Pool Repair Costs in Bali: What Actually Goes Into the Quote

There is no single 'pool repair price' in Bali because every villa is different: an old concrete shell, a new liner, metal fittings eaten by salt air, or a pump that finally gave up after three rainy seasons. The honest answer is that the cost depends entirely on what is broken, which parts need replacing, and whether the job touches plumbing, electrics, or the structure itself. Send a few photos of the problem and you will get a clear, itemised quote before anyone picks up a wrench.

Types of Work We Handle Most Often

In Sanur villas the most common calls are: cracked or popped tiles around the coping, a leaking skimmer or return jet, a pump motor that hums but does not turn, and filtration cartridges clogged beyond cleaning. Older pools built in the 1990s often have hairline cracks in the plaster that let water seep into the surrounding soil, especially once the dry season bakes the ground.

Metal ladders, handrails, and underwater lights corrode faster here than in inland climates because of the constant sea breeze. If you notice orange-brown streaks near fittings, that is salt-air corrosion eating through protective coatings — catch it early and the fix is small; ignore it for a year and the whole fitting may need replacing.

One safety note: if the pool electrical panel smells burnt, trips repeatedly, or you see any exposed wiring near the water line, stop using the pool immediately and call a licensed electrician before calling for a plumber. Water plus faulty electrics is not something to troubleshoot yourself.

  • Tile replacement and re-grouting
  • Pump, motor, and impeller swaps
  • Skimmer, return-jet, and plumbing leaks
  • Filtration system service (cartridge, sand, or DE)
  • Underwater light and ladder corrosion repair
  • Plaster crack sealing and waterproofing

How Diagnostics Works Before Any Quote

A proper quote starts with looking at the actual problem, not guessing from a description over the phone. The technician will run the pump, check pressure on the filter gauge, inspect the skimmer basket, and trace where water is actually escaping. For older pools they will also look behind the coping stones and under the deck slab, because Sanur's laterite soil shifts slightly each rainy season and can open new hairline cracks in the shell.

If your villa runs off a well rather than PDAM mains, the mineral content of that water matters. High iron or calcium levels accelerate scale build-up inside pipes and clog filters faster, which changes both the diagnosis and the parts you will need going forward.

The visit itself usually takes 30–60 minutes for a straightforward issue. More complex structural checks — say, a suspected leak below the water line — may require draining part of the pool and taking closer photos, so budget an extra hour.

Materials and Parts: What Drives the Cost

Parts are the biggest variable. A standard impeller seal kit, a set of replacement tiles matched to your existing colour, a new cartridge element, or a full pump motor all sit in very different price brackets. Some items are stocked locally in Bali hardware stores; others (certain motor sizes, specific tile formats) must be ordered from Java or imported, which adds lead time and cost.

Chemicals are another factor. If your pool has been running on chlorine tablets but you want to switch to a salt system, that is a separate installation job with its own parts list. And a word of caution: never mix chlorine-based products with acid or ammonia-based cleaners. The fumes can be dangerous. If you are unsure what went wrong after a chemical mishap, stop adding anything and call a professional to test the water before you swim again.

For older pools the grout and waterproofing compounds used will differ from a brand-new build. The technician will match whatever was originally specified where possible, because mixing incompatible sealants can cause peeling within a season.

How Long the Job Actually Takes

A single cracked tile, a stuck skimmer door, or a filter-cartridge swap can often be done the same day if the part is on hand. Pump-motor replacements usually take one working day including testing. Tile work over a larger area — say 10 to 20 tiles around the coping — typically runs two to three days because each batch of grout needs proper curing time in Bali's humidity.

The rainy season (roughly November through March) adds a wrinkle: exterior plaster work, re-grouting, and any job involving fresh cement or epoxy cannot be rushed under a downpour. If your repair falls in those months, plan for an extra day or two of buffer so the weather does not ruin a fresh coat.

Interior plumbing fixes inside the pump room are less weather-dependent, but access matters. In some Sanur villas the pump sits under a narrow stairwell or behind a garden wall; the more awkward the access, the longer the physical work takes even if the parts are simple.

Getting Your Quote: What to Send and What to Expect

There is no fixed price list, and anyone who hands you one without seeing the pool is guessing. The way it works here is straightforward: you send photos (and ideally a short video of the problem in action) on WhatsApp, the technician reviews them, and replies with what they see, which parts will likely be needed, and a cost range. If the issue is not obvious from photos, they will arrange a quick site visit before committing to a number.

To make that first message as useful as possible, include: roughly how old the pool is, whether it runs off PDAM mains or a well, what exactly went wrong (when did you first notice it?), and whether any recent chemical treatment or heavy rain preceded the problem. A blurry phone photo is perfectly fine — the goal is to triage, not to take an art class.

Once you approve the quote, the technician schedules the work, sources any missing parts, does the repair, and walks you through a quick test run so you can confirm everything looks right before they pack up tools.

  • Send 3–5 clear photos plus a 10-second video if the issue is active
  • Mention water source (PDAM / well) and approximate pool age
  • Ask for the quote broken into labour vs. parts so you understand where money goes

Questions

Do I have to pay just for someone to come and look?

The technician will tell you upfront whether a site-visit fee applies when no repair follows, or whether it is absorbed into the job price once you go ahead with the work. You will know the arrangement before anyone drives out to your villa, so there are no surprises at the gate.

My pool lost about 2 cm of water overnight. Is that normal in Sanur?

A small amount of evaporation is expected, especially in the dry season when humidity drops below 60 percent. But a consistent 2 cm drop every night usually points to a slow leak — often a hairline crack in the plaster or a weeping joint behind the coping. It is worth getting a pressure test done rather than topping up the level daily, because the water bill adds up fast over a Bali summer.

Can I patch a single cracked tile myself while waiting for the plumber?

For one or two tiles, a temporary silicone sealant from a hardware store can stop visible dripping for a day or two, but it is not a fix. The underlying cause — a shifted substrate, failed waterproofing membrane, or a crack running deeper into the shell — needs proper assessment. Avoid mixing random adhesives onto old grout lines; they can make the eventual professional repair harder and more expensive.
