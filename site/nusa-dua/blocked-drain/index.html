Blocked Drains & Toilets Nusa Dua – Fast, Chemical-Free Fixes

If your villa in Nusa Dua is dealing with a blocked toilet, a slow shower drain, or a foul smell rising from the floor waste, Bali Fix clears Blocked Drains & Toilets Nusa Dua properties using a flexible drain snake — no corrosive chemicals that eat through fittings or upset septic tanks. We work across Tanjung Benoa, Sawangan and the gated estates nearby, scheduling around complex access rules so there's no last-minute scramble.

Salt Air, Hard Water, and What They Do to Your Pipes

Nusa Dua sits right on the coast, so the salt-laden air accelerates corrosion on brass fittings, trap bends and septic-tank connections. Add Bukit water's mineral load and you get scale that slowly narrows every pipe diameter. That combination is why a drain that ran fine last year starts backing up this one. We clear these blockages with a flexible drain snake rather than caustic powders — the latter would strip the remaining protective layer off corroded joints and shorten your system's life further.

What Villa Owners Around Bualu and Sawangan Usually Message Us About

In the spacious villas near Bualu and Sawangan, bathrooms are large but the plumbing behind them is often original or only partially updated. The most common calls: a toilet that won't flush after a busy guest week, a shower drain that gurgles and backs up, a floor waste in the pool area reeking of stagnant water, or a sewer line that backs into two different fixtures at once. Each one gets diagnosed on site and cleared mechanically so the next clog doesn't arrive in three months.

Keeping Drains Clear Between Visits

A few habits go a long way in this climate. Fit hair catchers in every shower head — the salt air means guests use more body wash and conditioner, which clings to pipe walls. Never pour cooking oil down the kitchen sink; it solidifies inside the pipe within hours. Flush only human waste and paper. And if your villa sits in a gated estate like Tanjung Benoa's complexes, book a routine snake-through every six to eight months so scale never builds into a full blockage.
