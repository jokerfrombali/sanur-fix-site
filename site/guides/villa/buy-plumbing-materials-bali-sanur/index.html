Where to Buy Plumbing Fixtures & Materials in Bali

If your showerhead finally gave up or you need a new PVC coupling before the next rainy-season downpour, you have more sourcing options than most expats realise. Around Sanur and central Denpasar there are dedicated building-material shops, and increasingly you can order smaller fittings online. Here is how I usually advise clients to get what they actually need without overpaying or walking away with the wrong thread size.

Physical Shops Along Jalan Raya and Beyond

Start with the cluster of hardware and building-material stores along Jalan Raya Sanur and the stretch towards Jl. Bypass. You will find everything from small washers and silicone cartridges to rolls of PPR pipe, brass ball valves, and basic pool chemicals. Most of these are family-run, so the owner can often point you to the right fitting if you describe the problem clearly.

For larger items – rooftop water tanks, septic-tank components, pool pumps, or long runs of drainage pipe – head to the bigger yards further south near Kuta or along the bypass road. They stock full ranges and usually have a small office where someone can help you cross-reference sizes.

A practical tip: go in the morning. By mid-afternoon the best stock on specific valve diameters or unusual gasket sizes may already be spoken for, especially in dry season when renovation work peaks.

  • Carry a tape measure and a photo of the old part before you leave the villa.
  • Ask whether the item is rated for pressurised PDAM lines or only gravity-fed use.
  • Note the shop's phone number; many will hold an item for 24 hours while you confirm fit.

Ordering Online (Shopee, Tokopedia, Lazada)

Marketplace apps cover a surprisingly wide range of plumbing parts: O-rings, specific cartridge models, flexible hose connectors, even niche pool filter cartridges that local shops simply do not carry. The advantage is selection and the ability to compare several sellers side by side.

The downside is quality variance. A listing photo rarely tells you whether a brass fitting is solid or thinly plated, or whether a 'stainless' bracket is actually 304 grade. For anything under pressure or exposed to Sanur's salt air, I would rather buy in person where you can weigh the piece and check the stamping.

If you do order online, search by model number or take a clear close-up photo of the existing part. Most seller chat windows accept images, and a good seller will confirm compatibility before you pay.

What to Expect on Price

Pricing in Bali varies widely depending on whether a component was imported from China, manufactured locally, or brought in from Singapore. Two identical-looking ball valves from different shops can differ noticeably in cost because one is a bulk-import lot and the other is a single unit marked up for convenience.

I won't quote figures here – it depends entirely on the size, material, and whether you are buying one piece or a full kit. If you are unsure whether a price looks reasonable, send me a photo of the item and its label on WhatsApp; I can tell you if it falls in the normal range for that spec.

As a rule of thumb: compare at least two sources (one physical shop, one online listing) before committing, especially for items above a few hundred thousand rupiah.

Quality: What Actually Matters in Sanur

Two local factors affect which materials hold up over time. First, PDAM water in parts of Sanur carries fine sediment and can be moderately hard, so cheap unlined fittings corrode faster than expected. Second, the sea breeze accelerates oxidation on any exposed metal, particularly around pool decks and outdoor taps.

For pressurised hot-water lines I always recommend PPR or 304/316 stainless rather than standard PVC or thin brass. For pool plumbing, make sure every gasket and O-ring is rated for continuous chlorine exposure – generic rubber seals will crack within a season.

If you smell gas near a water heater or see any electrical wiring running close to a wet area while you are inspecting pipes, stop what you are doing and call a licensed professional. Do not attempt to test a suspected leak yourself with a flame or multimeter.

  • Look for '304' or '316' stamped on stainless components; avoid unmarked 'stainless'.
  • Check that pipe couplings are rated PN16 or higher if they sit on a PDAM feed line.
  • For pool parts, confirm chemical compatibility with your specific chlorine dose.

Getting It Delivered to Your Villa

Most shops within Denpasar city limits will arrange same-day delivery for a modest fee, especially if you are picking up more than one item. Call ahead and give your exact address including the villa name; some compounds in Sanur have multiple gates and it helps the driver not to circle the block twice.

Online orders typically arrive via GoSend, GrabExpress, or a courier within one to three working days depending on the seller's location. Bulky items like a 500-litre tank or a 20-metre pipe roll usually require a pickup arrangement or a negotiated truck delivery – factor an extra day into your timeline.

During the rainy season (roughly November to March) some side streets near the beach get muddy and slow. If you need materials urgently for a repair, buy locally rather than waiting for a courier that may be rerouted around flooded roads.

Questions

Can I just order everything online and skip the shop trip?

Small consumables – washers, silicone, O-rings, a single cartridge – work fine through Shopee or Tokopedia. But for anything structural, under pressure, or where thread size matters (valves, couplings, pump connections), I strongly recommend buying in person so you can physically check the diameter, material grade, and fit before paying.

Do shops in Sanur stock pool-specific parts like filter cartridges or heater elements?

Basic chlorine feeders, small filters, and generic pool chemicals are available at most hardware stores along Jalan Raya. For branded pump spares, specific filter cartridges, or heat-exchanger elements, your best bet is the larger yards near Bypass or searching by exact model number on an online marketplace.

Should I bring my old part with me when shopping?

Yes, always. Thread sizes, valve diameters, and fitting types vary even within the same nominal pipe size. A photo helps if you are ordering online, but holding the actual piece next to the new one eliminates guesswork and saves a second trip.
