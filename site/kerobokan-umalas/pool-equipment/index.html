Pool Equipment Kerobokan & Umalas – Pumps, Filters & Chlorinators

Families in Kerobokan Kelod and Umalas run their pools year-round, which means pumps, filters and chlorinators take a steady beating that holiday-rental pools simply don't. Bali Fix handles every pool equipment job residents here need — from a noisy pump that won't prime to a full salt-chlorinator swap or a multiport valve that no longer holds position.

How Local Conditions Wear Down Your Pool Gear

The high water table around Kerobokan and Batu Belig keeps septic lines and garden drains under constant pressure, and that moisture creeps into pump rooms faster than you'd expect. Long-term families also push circulation pumps and heaters far harder than short-stay guests do, so seals, bearings and valves wear on a predictable schedule. Older villas sitting beside newer builds mean both corroded copper fittings and fresh PVC joints show up in the same repair.

Typical Jobs We See Around This Stretch of Coast

The most common calls are straightforward: a pump that lost prime after a power cut, sand filters turning cloudy despite backwashing, salt chlorinators throwing error codes, and underwater lights flickering mid-evening. In Umalas, where many pools feed larger garden irrigation loops, a single stuck auto-fill valve can quietly drain a tank overnight. Each is fixable on-site; what matters is catching the symptom before it takes out the motor.

What to Send on WhatsApp for an Accurate Quote

Snap two or three photos of your pump room — the unit with its model plate visible, the filter housing, and any error code on the chlorinator controller. A short clip of the noise or water colour helps too. Send them over WhatsApp along with your neighbourhood and a one-line note on what changed. Because pricing depends entirely on the specific job, you'll receive a firm quote once we've reviewed the images.

Prevention Habits That Save Trouble in Kerobokan & Umalas

Check your auto-fill float every month — a stuck valve is the number-one cause of overnight overflow into garden beds. Backwash the sand filter on schedule rather than waiting for pressure to spike, and flush the multiport valve after each season change. If your pool sits near an irrigation line, clear the drain outlet of root growth regularly; the high groundwater here means clogged drains back up fast during wet months.

Questions

How do I know if my filter sand needs replacing?

It varies with how heavily the pool is used and local water conditions. A quick photo of the sand bed sent over WhatsApp will let us judge whether replacement is due before you commit to a job.

Can you fix a salt chlorinator that keeps resetting itself?

Yes — most resets trace back to a fouled cell, low brine level, or a wiring fault at the controller. Send a photo of the error code plus the unit's model number via WhatsApp and we'll confirm the fix and quote from there.

Do you cover Batu Belig as well as Kerobokan Kelod and Umalas?

Absolutely; our pool equipment work spans all three neighbourhoods and the surrounding lanes. Just message us on WhatsApp with your address and what's acting up, and we'll sort it out.
