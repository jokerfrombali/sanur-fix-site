Local knowledge

What's different in Jimbaran

Jimbaran mixes bay-side homes and hillside villas on the edge of the Bukit — both salt air and water-supply issues show up here.

Hillside water pressure

Villas up the hill depend on tanks and pumps; low pressure and pumps cycling are frequent.

Family villas with pools

Larger family homes mean more bathrooms, bigger pools and more equipment to maintain.

Neighbourhoods I cover in Jimbaran: Jimbaran Bay, Kedonganan, Bukit Jimbaran, Goa Gong, Kelan.
