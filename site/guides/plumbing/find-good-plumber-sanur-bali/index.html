If your kitchen tap drips, the rooftop tank leaks, or the pool pump has stopped, you need someone who understands Sanur's specific setup — PDAM mains, well water, corroded pipework in older villas, and constant salt air. This guide covers where to find a reliable technician, how to vet them before they arrive, what drives the price, and the small mistakes that turn a quick fix into an expensive headache.

Where to Find a Plumber in Sanur

Start with people already living here. Ask your villa manager, neighbours in the compound, or other long-term tenants who have dealt with plumbing issues recently. A name passed along by someone who watched the work done on-site beats any online listing.

Sanur WhatsApp groups are also useful. Post a short description of the problem and ask for recommendations from residents who have used a particular technician. You will usually get honest feedback within a day or two.

Avoid random roadside signs or unverified listings. In a small area like Sanur, reputation travels fast, and a good technician does not need to advertise loudly.

  • Ask your villa manager or security guard for a name they trust
  • Post in local Sanur resident WhatsApp groups for first-hand references
  • Prefer someone based in or very close to Sanur so travel time is minimal

How to Check a Technician Before Calling

Before you book, spend five minutes asking the right questions over WhatsApp or a quick phone call. Request photos or a short video of similar jobs — a leaking under-sink pipe, a septic tank desludging, a pool pump replacement. A technician comfortable showing past work will send them without hesitation; if they cannot produce any examples, that is a red flag.

Confirm they carry common parts (flexi hoses, washers, check valves) in their van. In Sanur some older villas use fittings no longer stocked at every hardware shop, so a well-equipped technician saves you a second trip.

Also ask whether they handle both household plumbing and pool equipment. Many Sanur properties need both, and one call-out for two issues is more convenient than booking separately.

  • Request 2–3 recent job photos relevant to your specific problem
  • Check if they cover pool pumps, filters, and chlorine systems as well
  • Ask how they deal with PDAM water quality issues such as sediment or pressure drops

What It Costs and What Affects the Price

There is no single 'plumber rate' in Bali. The final price depends on the specific job, the parts needed, how hard it is to reach the pipe or tank, and whether the work falls outside normal hours. A straightforward tap replacement costs very differently from clearing a blocked septic line under a concrete slab, and a rooftop tank repair takes longer than a ground-level fix because of access time.

The most practical approach: send a clear photo or short video of the issue on WhatsApp. A good technician will give you a rough range before arriving, so there are no surprises at the door. If the scope changes once they open up a wall or lift the tank lid, they should explain why and agree with you before proceeding.

  • Parts (valves, hoses, pump seals) are billed separately from labour
  • Urgent call-outs during peak rainy season may carry a small surcharge
  • Ask for an itemised breakdown after the job listing each part by name and quantity

What to Prepare Before the Technician Arrives

You do not need to be a plumber yourself, but a few minutes of preparation makes the visit faster and cheaper. If it is safe to do so, shut off the water supply to the affected area — close the angle valve under a kitchen cabinet, or turn off the main PDAM / well-water valve near the meter or rooftop tank inlet.

Clear the space around the problem. Move chairs, towels, or pool chemicals away from the work zone. If the issue is in the septic area, note when the tank was last pumped and whether drainage has been slow or odours have appeared. Have your PDAM account number handy if the repair touches the external connection.

  • Photograph the exact spot before the technician arrives; this helps confirm the quote
  • Note when the problem started and whether it worsens at certain times of day
  • For pool issues, record current chlorine level and approximate pump runtime

Common Mistakes When Choosing a Plumber

Picking the cheapest name on a list without checking what is included. A low labour figure that excludes parts, travel, or access time often ends up costing more than a mid-range all-inclusive quote. Ignoring the age of the villa's pipework is another trap: many Sanur properties built in the 1990s and early 2000s have joints that corrode faster because of salt air, so a quick patch may hold for a month while replacing the section properly saves repeated call-outs.

Waiting until the rainy season to fix a small leak is perhaps the most expensive mistake. What drips in June becomes a flooded ceiling in November when every storm tests weak joints and overloaded drains. And never mix chlorine products yourself after a pool issue — if you smell strong fumes or see greenish-yellow gas, stop immediately, ventilate, and call a professional.

  • Do not attempt repairs on gas lines near water heaters; shut off the supply and call a licensed technician
  • Avoid DIY fixes on PDAM external connections, which can affect your supply agreement
  • Get any scope change agreed by WhatsApp message before extra work begins

Questions

Do I need a permit to replace pipes inside my Sanur villa?

For internal repairs and replacements within your property, no special permit is needed. However, if the work touches the PDAM external connection line or involves altering the septic tank structure, local coordination may be required. Your technician will tell you if that applies to your specific job.

Can one person handle both household plumbing and pool maintenance?

In Sanur many technicians cover both because the systems share the same water source and drainage. Confirm with the person you book whether they service pumps, filters, and chlorine dosing as well as taps, toilets, and tanks, so you only manage one contact for the whole property.

What should I do right now if I smell gas near the water heater?

Stop what you are doing. Do not flip light switches or use any electrical appliance nearby. Open windows and doors to ventilate, leave the area, and call a professional from outside the building. Do not attempt to locate the leak yourself.
